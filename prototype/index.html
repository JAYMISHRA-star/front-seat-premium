What the feature changes

  • Seat map at booking. Riders see the car from above and pick a seat, the way they pick a seat on a flight.
  • Three price tiers. The front seat costs more, window seats stay at the driver's price, and centre and back-row seats can cost less.
  • Driver control. Drivers opt in, set the premium within a band and see each seat's earnings before they publish.
  • In-app payment. A reserved seat needs a paid booking. That gives riders a reason to pay in the app, and gives the platform a booking fee it does not collect in India today.
  • Free safety switch. A rider can move from the front seat to any free back seat at pickup and get the difference back.
