# Rider survey: seat preferences on intercity carpools

Copy these questions into a Google Form. It takes about 4 minutes to complete. Target: **at least 100 responses** from people who have taken an intercity carpool or shared cab in the last 12 months.

**Where to share it:** BlaBlaCar riders you drive (with a QR code in the car), college and alumni WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn, Reddit r/india and city subreddits, and travel or commuter groups for corridors like Indore–Bhopal or Delhi–Jaipur.

**Form intro text (paste as the form description):**
> I'm Jay Mishra, an MBA student researching how people choose seats on shared intercity rides. This survey is anonymous and takes about 4 minutes. Answers are used only for a student case study.

---

## Section 1. Screening

1. In the last 12 months, how many times have you travelled between cities in a shared car (BlaBlaCar, shared cab, or carpool with people you didn't know)?
   - 0 *(end the form here: "Thanks, this survey is for people who have taken shared rides.")*
   - 1–2
   - 3–5
   - 6 or more
2. Which app or method did you use most? *(multiple choice + Other)* BlaBlaCar / Shared cab via a taxi app / WhatsApp or Facebook group / Through friends / Other

## Section 2. Seat experience

3. On your last shared ride, where did you sit? Front / Back window / Back middle / Third row (7-seater) / Don't remember
4. How was your seat decided? First to arrive / Driver chose / Passengers agreed among themselves / I asked for it / Other
5. If you could choose any seat, which would you pick? Front / Back window / Back middle / Third row / No preference
6. Why? *(checkboxes)* Legroom / Motion sickness / View / Privacy / Feel safer / Easier to get in and out / Phone charging / Want to sleep / Other
7. Have you ever felt uncomfortable about the seat you were given? Yes / No. *If yes:* What happened? *(short answer)*

## Section 3. Willingness to pay (Van Westendorp)

Set the scene in the section description: *"Imagine a ₹400 shared ride from Indore to Bhopal. The app lets you reserve the front seat for an extra charge."*

8. At what extra amount would reserving the front seat be **so cheap** that you'd doubt the offer? ₹ ___
9. At what extra amount would it be **a good deal**? ₹ ___
10. At what extra amount would it start to feel **expensive, but you'd still consider it**? ₹ ___
11. At what extra amount would it be **too expensive** to consider? ₹ ___

12. If the back middle seat were cheaper, how much of a discount would make you choose it on purpose? ₹0 (never) / ₹20 / ₹40 / ₹60 / ₹80 or more

## Section 4. Payment and trust

13. How did you pay for your last shared ride? Cash to driver / UPI to driver / In the app / Other
14. Would you pay in the app, plus a ₹15 booking fee, if it guaranteed your seat? Definitely / Probably / Not sure / Probably not / Definitely not
15. If you had booked the front seat, would you want the option to move to the back at pickup, for free? Yes, important / Nice to have / Don't care

## Section 5. About you (optional)

16. Age group: Under 22 / 22–30 / 31–45 / Over 45
17. Gender: Woman / Man / Non-binary / Prefer not to say
18. Height: Under 5'6" / 5'6"–6'0" / Over 6'0" / Prefer not to say
19. Would you be open to a 15-minute call about your experience? *(If yes, leave an email address. It is used only to schedule the call.)*

## How to analyse it (for doc 01 v2)

- **Share of riders who prefer the front seat**, split by gender, height and motion sickness.
- **Van Westendorp chart:** plot the cumulative curves for Q8–Q11. Where "too cheap" crosses "too expensive" gives the optimal price point. This sets the premium band in doc 03 with real data.
- **Q12:** how big a discount the saver seats need.
- **Q14:** the in-app payment share to use in the platform revenue model.
- **Q15 by gender:** evidence for or against the safety switch.
