# Interview guide

Use this for 15-minute calls. Aim for **8–10 riders and 5 drivers**, including at least 3 solo women riders. Ask about past behaviour, not about future intentions ("tell me about the last time…", not "would you…").

Before starting, ask for permission to take notes. Don't record without explicit consent. Store notes without full names.

## Riders

1. Tell me about the last time you took a shared intercity ride. Where were you going, and how did you book it?
2. Walk me through the moment you got to the car. How did you end up in your seat?
3. Has a seat ever made a ride noticeably better or worse? What happened?
4. Have you ever asked for a specific seat? How did that go?
5. Have you ever paid extra for a seat anywhere, like on a flight, at the cinema or on a bus? What made it worth it?
6. *(Show the prototype on your phone.)* What do you notice first? What would you tap?
7. What would make you not trust this?
8. *(For women riders, only if they're comfortable:)* Does who the driver is change where you'd like to sit?

## Drivers

1. How do passengers usually decide who sits where in your car?
2. Has a seat ever caused an argument or a complaint?
3. *(Show the driver panel.)* Would you turn this on? Which mode would you pick, and why?
4. What premium would feel fair to charge for the front seat on your usual route?
5. How do your passengers pay you today? Would in-app payment help or hurt you?

## After each interview

Write down within an hour: 3 direct quotes, the biggest surprise, and anything that contradicts a hypothesis in doc 01. These quotes go into the README as evidence.
